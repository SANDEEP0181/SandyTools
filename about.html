About SandyTools

SandyTools is a free online tools website created to provide simple, useful and easy-to-use tools for everyday tasks.

Our collection includes calculators, image tools, QR code tools, PDF utilities, text tools and content creation tools.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make common online tasks faster and easier without requiring complicated software or installations.

Free and Easy to Use

SandyTools is designed with a simple interface so that users can quickly find a tool, enter their information and get useful results.

Our Tools

SandyTools currently provides a variety of free tools including percentage, age, EMI, GST, SIP and discount calculators, image compression and resizing tools, QR code generation, PDF utilities, text tools and YouTube creator tools.
