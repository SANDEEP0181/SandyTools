Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SandyTools handles information when visitors use the website and its browser-based tools.

Information We Collect

Most SandyTools tools are designed to process entered text, dates, numbers or selected files directly in the browser. SandyTools does not intentionally provide a server-side feature for storing ordinary tool inputs unless a feature clearly states otherwise.

Website infrastructure, analytics or advertising services may process technical information such as browser, device, approximate location, pages visited and interaction information according to their own policies and configurations.

Browser-Based Processing

Calculators, text utilities, image processing and PDF merging can perform their main operations in your browser. Files selected for browser-based processing are not intentionally uploaded by these tools as part of the normal calculation or processing flow. You should still review the behavior of any third-party service used by a specific feature.

Third-Party Services

SandyTools may use third-party libraries or services for specific functionality. For example, the QR generator currently requests QR image generation from a third-party QR service, and the PDF merger uses a browser JavaScript library loaded from a public content delivery network. Third-party providers may process requests or technical information under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and Advertising

If advertising or analytics services are enabled, cookies or similar technologies may be used. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies to provide, measure or personalize advertising according to their applicable policies and user settings.

Users can manage personalized advertising choices through the controls provided by Google and their browser or device. Available controls can vary by country, browser and service.

External Links

SandyTools may link to external websites or services. We do not control those websites and recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

Children's Privacy

SandyTools is a general-purpose website and does not intentionally request personal information from children through its tools.

Data Security

No website can guarantee absolute security. Avoid entering passwords, payment credentials, private keys or other highly sensitive information into general-purpose online tools.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, advertising, analytics or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact page.
